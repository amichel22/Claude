Now MR vascular fingerprinting, the part closest to my project. This is the GESFIDE sequence the lab uses, in a figure adapted from Ni and colleagues. In part A, after the 90-degree pulse, gradient echoes sample the free induction decay. The signal falls with R2 star, which includes dephasing from static fields. In part B, after the 180-degree pulse at 50 milliseconds, the phase from static fields starts to unwind, and it's refocused at the spin echo at 100 milliseconds. T2 decay keeps going, so the signal still falls, just more slowly. Comparing the slopes in parts A and B is how GESFIDE separates R2 from R2 prime. After the spin echo, in part C, dephasing builds again. White matter, gray matter and CSF already follow different curves. The question is how this curve carries vessel information.
