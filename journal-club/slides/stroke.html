Moving to clinical results. This is MRF-ASL in 34 ischemic stroke patients, from Hongli Fan and colleagues in Hanzhang Lu's group. Each row is one patient, with stroke severity increasing going down. Lesions are outlined in green and normal tissue in blue. In the lesions, one-compartment CBF was about 15 versus about 29 in normal tissue, and arrival time was about 1.5 seconds versus 0.9. These values tracked stroke severity, and MRF-ASL classified lesions better than pCASL. In Moyamoya disease, Su and colleagues found longer arrival times behind the narrowed arteries. CBF stayed normal there and went up in the posterior circulation, which the paper reads as collateral flow.
