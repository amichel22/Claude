For tumors, this figure compares MRvF with vessel size imaging in glioma patients, from Venugopal and colleagues. The radius and blood volume maps look similar, and Venugopal reported MRvF as more robust to noise, with higher resolution. In rat glioma models, Lemasson and colleagues found that 9L tumors had SO2 around 30 percent, compared with 45 to 50 percent in the C6 and F98 models. Conventional quantitative BOLD, or qBOLD, overestimated it at 40 to 50 percent. And in atherosclerotic mice, Pouliot found larger vessels, more blood volume and lower oxygen saturation than in wild-type mice. So MRvF picks up vessel remodeling, not only tumors.
