The paper sorts the field into three families, and the rest of it follows this order. MRF-ASL randomizes the labeling and fits a kinetic model to get CBF and bolus arrival time. MR vascular fingerprinting, or MRvF, gets blood volume, mean vessel radius and SO2 from gradient and spin echo signals, using a Monte Carlo model of vessels. Repeated during a vascular challenge, it also gives cerebrovascular reactivity. The third, MRF-VFD, for vascular fluid dynamics, targets vessel function and permeability, like Ktrans and water exchange across the barrier. Many of these methods also give supporting maps like T1, T2 and B1.
