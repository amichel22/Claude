My take, and questions for discussion

What I found most useful

One framework for ASL, vascular and fluid-dynamics MRF: acquisition, dictionary, matching.

Table 2 works as a lookup: sequence, dictionary ranges, outputs and matching method for each study.

It is direct about the limits: SNR, field bias, and dictionaries built for healthy tissue.

Questions I have

1

Besides PET, what does the lab use as a reference for vessel radius and SO₂ in humans?

2

The dictionary depends on how it was simulated (BOLDsωimsuite shows methods differ in radius error). Should dictionaries be benchmarked and versioned like sequences?

3
