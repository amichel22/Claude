What still limits physiological MRF

1.

Field inhomogeneity. B0 and B1+ errors bias the fit. Map them and add them to the dictionary, use sequences less sensitive to B0 (FISP, FLASH rather than bSSFP), or correct with deep learning.

2.

Signal-to-noise. MRvF needs an SNR of about 20 or more to match well (Wheeler). In ASL the label is only about 1% of the signal.

3.

Model assumptions. Dictionaries are usually built for healthy tissue and simplified models. In disease the real signal can sit outside every entry, which biases the match.

4.

Validation and standards. Static phantoms, then flow phantoms, then in vivo against PET. Sites need shared protocols (HARP, QIBA).

Directions the review points to: brain water exchange and glymphatic flow with multi-echo or long-TE ASL, and simultaneous PET/MR.
