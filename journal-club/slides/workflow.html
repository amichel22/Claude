Physiological MRF puts physiology into the dictionary

Lin, Lu & Fan, Magn Reson Med 2025, Fig. 1

5

This is Figure 1 of the paper, and it frames the whole review. The matching idea is the same as standard MRF, but two things change. First, the acquisition is built to be sensitive to physiology, like blood flow, oxygenation or water exchange, not just T1 and T2. Second, the dictionary comes from a biophysical model. Here the model is a virtual voxel of vessels, over the ranges of oxygen saturation, blood volume and radius shown at the bottom left. Each voxel's signal is matched to that dictionary, and you get maps of SO2, CBV and vessel radius.
