Why this matters for our dictionaries

1.

MrVox uses the 3B0 shortcut, with an FFT field and grid diffusion. It made the dictionary for the first MRvF paper (Christen 2014) and several since.

2.

MrVox contributors found that 3B0 overestimated vessel radius by 14–⁠19% in rats, compared with a dictionary from realistic vessel networks (Delphin 2024).

3.

Berman's conclusion: 3D-ANA-MC is the attractive default, and 2D-ANA-MC reproduced it almost exactly for random cylinders. 3B0 "should be used with caution."

The gap

Berman tested only vessel radius, against a known answer. Delphin's group compared dictionaries on GESFIDE in rats, where the true values are unknown. A known-answer test of 3B0 for CBV, R and SO₂ on our 38-echo GESFIDE is still missing.
