Why this matters for our dictionaries

1.

MrVox uses the 3B0 shortcut, with an FFT field and grid diffusion. It made the dictionary for the first MRvF paper (Christen 2014) and several since.

2.

MrVox contributors found that 3B0 overestimated vessel radius by 14–⁠19% in rats, compared with a dictionary from realistic vessel networks (Delphin 2024).

3.

Berman's advice: use 3D-ANA-MC, or 2D-ANA-MC, which matched it almost exactly. Use 3B0 with caution.

Not tested yet

Berman only checked vessel radius, with plain gradient- and spin-echo signals at one blood volume. Nobody has checked our 38-echo GESFIDE, or what 3B0 does to CBV and SO₂.
