This is the figure that made the toolbox click for me. Every simulation is four choices, left to right. Geometry: is the voxel 2D or 3D, and are the vessels straight cylinders, spheres, or a realistic vessel network. The field: do you use the exact formula for a cylinder, or a Fourier transform on a grid, which works for any shape but is approximate. Diffusion: do you track thousands of random water molecules, which is Monte Carlo, or blur the magnetization on a grid, which is deterministic diffusion. And the signal: which pulse sequence you play, for us the 38-echo GESFIDE. The method name just spells out the choices. 3D-ANA-MC means 3D, analytical field, Monte Carlo. MrVox, the older Matlab tool, is one fixed combination: 2D, FFT, grid diffusion, plus a shortcut called 3B0 that I'll come back to, because it turns out to matter.
