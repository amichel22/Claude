Before I go on, I want to answer the three questions from last week. I spent this week making sure I understand them. First, you asked whether MrVox computes the field analytically or with an FFT. It's the FFT. MrVox works on a 2D grid, gets the field with a Fourier method, moves the water with grid diffusion, and averages the field over three B0 directions, the 3B0 shortcut. Christen's 2014 paper describes the Fourier field and the grid diffusion, and Chaussé and Berman both say MrVox also uses the 3B0 average. Analytical or FFT only describes how the field is computed; MrVox is a numerical simulation either way. One subtlety: Berman's 3B0 test used the exact field and Monte Carlo, so the errors on the 3B0 panel come from the averaging, not from the FFT. The FFT recipes only missed two radii each.
