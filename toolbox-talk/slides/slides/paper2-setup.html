The second paper uses the toolbox to ask the question that matters for us: does the recipe change the answer? They took eight approaches people have published and ran all of them with the same settings: 3 tesla, 2 percent blood volume, fifteen vessel sizes from 1 to 60 microns, and ten random voxels. The figure shows the geometries. From left: a single vessel in 2D, many vessels in 2D, either each with its own field direction or with a three-direction average, random cylinders in 3D, and a simplified vessel network with branching capillaries. The reference is 3D cylinders with the exact field and Monte Carlo diffusion. They compared everything in three ways, and the one closest to what we do is a mini fingerprinting test: use each approach as the dictionary and see if you get the right vessel radius back.
