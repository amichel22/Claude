2

First, why we need a simulator at all. With vascular fingerprinting, the scanner gives us a decay curve for each voxel, 38 echoes from GESFIDE. On its own, that curve doesn't tell us blood volume or vessel size. So we build a dictionary: simulated curves for every combination of oxygenation, blood volume and vessel radius. Then we match each measured curve to the closest simulated one and read off the parameters. That gives us the maps. The key point is on the bottom. The maps can only be as right as the simulated curves. If the simulation has a bias, the maps inherit it in a systematic way, and the matching step has no way of flagging it. The curve still matches well; it's just the wrong curve. BOLD swim suite is the tool that makes those simulated curves.
